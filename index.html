Case studies

Map SDK rescue: the release blocker 10 screens to Compose · 1.5 months
Context
Our forked map SDK was outdated and no longer supported by its maintainers, and its native libraries didn't meet Google Play's 16 KB page-size requirement. We couldn't keep it in production: new features were blocked, and customers were threatening to churn.
What I did
Researched community alternatives and chose a map SDK that worked with our existing map infrastructure. Switching meant rewriting every map screen from XML to Jetpack Compose, across both core products on phone and tablet.
Outcome
Shipped in 1.5 months: 10 screens across people transport and logistics (2 + 2 on phone, 3 + 3 on tablet). Releases resumed, and the company went back to shipping features and earning revenue.

Build speed & code-quality foundations Builds 8–10 → 3–4 min · 80% test coverage
Context
Gradle builds took 8–10 minutes. Features were tightly coupled with no separation of concerns, which made the app hard to scale or maintain. There were no unit tests and no documentation, and this was before AI coding assistants.
What I did
Split the app into multiple modules, optimised the Gradle scripts, built up unit tests, added Detekt for lint checks, and built a CI/CD pipeline that runs the tests and publishes with Fastlane.
Outcome
Builds dropped to 3–4 minutes. Unit-test coverage reached 80%, verified with JaCoCo reports. Detekt enforces a shared coding style, and one command bumps the version, runs the tests and publishes to staging or production.

How I work

Delivery Framework

  1. Step 1 · Architectural Foundations

    Stack Selection & CI/CD Pipeline

    Choose the right stack (Native Android/iOS vs. React Native / Expo) based on product requirements, performance needs, and timeline. Establish modular architecture, CI/CD automation, crash telemetry, and lint checks before feature development begins.

  2. Step 2 · Iterative MVP Delivery

    Continuous Builds & Store Releases

    Ship predictable, testable builds on physical devices. Integrate backend services (Supabase, Firebase, or custom APIs) with offline resilience. Take features through store compliance and release to Google Play and the App Store.

  3. Step 3 · Reliability & Scale

    Production Monitoring & Optimization

    Monitor real-world crash-free rates, network latency, and memory footprint. Refactor hotspots, modularize subsystems to maintain rapid build times, and automate regression coverage as feature scope grows.

Engineering Principles

  • AI as a multiplier, verified by engineering rigor

    I integrate AI workflows daily for test generation, boilerplate, and triage — but every line of code is human-verified and anchored in sound architecture.

  • Every release through an automated pipeline

    Releases are automated via Fastlane and CI/CD pipelines, never built ad-hoc from an individual laptop. Every build is reproducible, tracked, and tested.

  • Real-device validation over emulator assumptions

    Testing prioritizes physical devices to catch OEM-specific background limits, battery throttling, and intermittent connectivity that emulators hide.
