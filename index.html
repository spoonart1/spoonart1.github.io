How I work

Stability is a feature

A crash rate is a product decision that someone has decided not to make. I treat crash-free rate as a number the team owns and reports on, which is the only reason 82% ever becomes 99.3%.

I leave the pipeline behind

I have built the first CI/CD pipeline at two companies now. Shipping a feature helps for a sprint; making releases boring and repeatable helps for years after I have moved on.

AI agents, with judgment intact

I build agents for the work that rewards repetition — UI automation, crash triage, first-pass review. The goal is not to replace engineering judgment. It is to stop spending it on things that never needed it.
